# Scoring & signal diagnostics — 2026-04-23

## TL;DR

The scoring function has three separate defects that combine to make the
emitted signals *anti*-predictive in the dominant output range:

1. **`decidedROI` ≠ signal EV.** It measures overall trading skill
   (including exits, scaling, hedging). Wallets with high ROI often have
   alpha that we can't capture by copying their entries. Attribution
   multiplier (just shipped) fixes this retroactively.
2. **`mmPenalty` is broken.** mmScore > 0 should multiply score by < 1,
   but for wallets with mmScore 1 and 2 the stored `mmPenalty` is 1.00 —
   the classifier flags them but the penalty never bites.
3. **`confidence` / `tier = elite` / `activityBonus` are inverted.** Higher
   values correlate with *worse* signal outcomes. Likely because they
   combine walletCount (good) with avgScore (bad → defect #1).

### 5. Attribution blind spot

Of 1446 resolved signals, only **692 are attributable** — cluster and
consensus signals (759 total) don't persist their contributing wallet
addresses. The attribution feedback loop can't close on 52% of signals
until the emitter is updated to save wallet lists.

## Remediation roadmap (ranked by EV per hour)

1. **Fix `mmPenalty` bug.** Pure bug, quick fix. Inspect scoring code,
   wire `mmPenalty = max(0.5, 1 - mmScore × 0.15)` (or whatever the
   intended formula was — it's currently always returning 1.0).
2. **Add category whitelist as signal gate.** +20 points per dollar
   deployed in backtest. Implement as a new `ALLOWED_CATEGORIES` constant
   + check in `emitSignal()`, cheap to add.
3. **Deprecate `tier = elite` promotion / flip to neutral.** Currently
   −14.7% weighted return. Change `tier` threshold math or remove as a
   sizing knob.
4. **Cap or invert the confidence contribution above ~70.** Beyond 70,
   confidence is anti-predictive. Either clip at 70 or replace with
   pure walletCount (which is positive standalone).
5. **Persist wallet addresses on cluster / consensus signals.** Unblocks
   attribution on 759 signals. Add `wallets: [...]` to emitSignal.
6. **Backfill missing specialists.** 6–10 wallets with strong per-category
   history aren't in the pool. Add them via backfill-pool with an
   attribution-driven whitelist.
7. **Add `churnPenalty`.** Multiplier = `min(1, 3 / (totalTrades/uniqueMarkets))`.
   Penalises rebalancers (wallet B style) who churn 9× per market.
   Wait until attribution has cycled through a couple scans before layering.
8. **Widen category classifier.** 821 "other" signals are too big a
   bucket — split it into token-launches, news-events, conditional-markets,
   etc. and measure each.
